Questions

Who counts as an active committer?

A distinct author email (folded through .mailmap) with at least one commit authored in the last 30 days, among the commits your pipeline builds. Bots such as Dependabot, Renovate and github-actions are excluded, and you can add your own service accounts. td license status shows the count and the bot list, computed on your machines; no names or emails are sent anywhere.

What happens if we go over our seats?

Up to 20% over (at least one person) is fine. Above that, {{name}} keeps working and warns in every job summary for 30 days. Only if you are still over after that does it run every test, as it would without a license. It never fails a build.

What happens when the license expires?

Warnings start 14 days before. After the end date every test runs again, exactly as before you installed {{name}}. Builds keep passing or failing on their own merits.

Does {{name}} send our code anywhere?

No. Analysis, coverage maps and test runs stay in your CI. {{name}}'s only network call of its own is an optional license refresh that sends the license itself, at most once a day per machine (once per job on CI runners that start fresh); the GitHub Action also downloads the {{name}} package from nuget.org. Set TESTDETTA_LICENSE_REFRESH=off to turn the refresh off.

Who handles payment and invoices?

{{seller}} is our merchant of record: it takes payment, charges the right sales tax or VAT, and issues invoices. Your license arrives by email right after checkout.
