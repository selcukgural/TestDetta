The {{name}} tool

{{name}} runs inside your CI and on your developers' machines, and makes no network calls of its own. It reads no license and sends no usage data. It does not send source code, diffs, coverage maps, test names, test results or build output to us or to anyone else. In GitHub Actions, the {{name}} action keeps the coverage maps in your repository's Actions cache and the shadow and audit reports as artifacts of your workflow runs, inside your GitHub account. The package is downloaded from nuget.org, under nuget.org's own terms and privacy policy. We process no personal data through the tool.

Sponsoring

Sponsorships go through GitHub Sponsors. GitHub takes the payment under its own privacy statement; we never see card details. GitHub shows us which account sponsors and with which tier, and shares other details only as GitHub's settings and the sponsor's choices allow. We use this only to manage sponsorships and to keep the records tax law requires.

Legal basis: the sponsorship you entered into (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b); KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)) and our legal obligations (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c); KVKK Art. 5(2)(ç)).

Support

When you email us, we keep the conversation in our mailbox to help you and to answer later questions: your address, your name if you give it, and what you write.

Legal basis: answering your request (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and (f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(c) and (f)).

This website

The site uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking scripts, and loads nothing from other sites. If you switch between the light and dark theme, your choice is kept in your own browser's local storage so the next page opens the same way; it never leaves your browser.

Our hosting provider processes request data such as IP addresses to serve pages and protect against abuse, under its own terms. We receive no request logs and keep none.

Legal basis for the request data: our legitimate interest in delivering a working and secure site (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)).

Who processes data for us

  • GitHub (Microsoft): the source repository and GitHub Sponsors.
  • Google: this website (Firebase Hosting and its content delivery network) and our mailbox (Gmail).
  • Porkbun: our domain, and forwarding mail sent to {{supportEmail}} to our mailbox.
  • Resend: sending our replies from {{supportEmail}}.

Most of them operate in the United States, outside {{licensorCountry}} and the EU. Transfers there rely on the safeguards the law requires, such as standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision (for the United States, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework).

How long we keep it

  • Support email: as long as needed to help you and answer follow-up questions; deleted earlier when you ask.
  • Sponsorship records: while the sponsorship runs, and afterwards for the period Turkish tax law requires records to be kept (five years from the end of the year they concern).

Your rights

Under the GDPR, the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK, Art. 11) and similar laws, you can ask whether we process your data and for a copy of it, ask for its correction, deletion, restriction or transfer to you, learn who it was shared with, and object to its use. Email {{supportEmail}}; we answer within 30 days. You can also complain to your data protection authority, in Türkiye the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK).
