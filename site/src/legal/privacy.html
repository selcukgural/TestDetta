The {{name}} tool

{{name}} runs inside your CI and on your developers' machines, and makes no network calls of its own. It reads no license and sends no usage data. It does not send source code, diffs, coverage maps, test names, test results or build output to us or to anyone else. In GitHub Actions, the {{name}} action keeps the coverage maps in your repository's Actions cache and the shadow and audit reports as artifacts of your workflow runs, inside your GitHub account. The package is downloaded from nuget.org, under nuget.org's own terms and privacy policy.

Sponsoring

Sponsorships go through GitHub Sponsors. GitHub takes the payment under its own privacy statement; we never see card details. GitHub shows us which account sponsors and with which tier, and shares other details only as GitHub's settings and the sponsor's choices allow. We use this only to manage sponsorships.

Support

When you email us, we keep the conversation in our mailbox to help you and to answer later questions. Our email provider processes those messages on our behalf.

This website

The site uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking scripts. Our hosting provider processes request data such as IP addresses to serve pages and protect against abuse, and keeps it only briefly.

Who processes data for us

  • GitHub: the source repository and GitHub Sponsors.
  • Our email and hosting providers: support mail and this website.

Some of them operate outside {{licensorCountry}} and the EU. Where that is the case, transfers rely on the safeguards the law requires.

How long we keep it

  • Support email: as long as needed to help you, then deleted on request.
  • Sponsorship records: while the sponsorship runs, and afterwards as long as tax and accounting law requires.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) and similar laws, you can ask for a copy of your data, its correction or deletion, and object to its use. Email {{supportEmail}}. You can also complain to your data protection authority.
