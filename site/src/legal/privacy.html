Privacy policy

The {{name}} tool

{{name}} runs inside your CI and on your developers' machines. It does not send source code, diffs, coverage maps, test names, test results or build output to us or to anyone else. In GitHub Actions, the {{name}} action keeps the coverage maps in your repository's Actions cache and the shadow and audit reports as artifacts of your workflow runs, inside your GitHub account.

  • Committers. To check the seat count, {{name}} counts distinct commit author emails locally. Emails and names are never sent, stored by us, or written to logs.
  • License check. The license is verified offline, with a public key built into {{name}}.
  • License refresh. At most once a day per machine (once per job on CI runners that start with an empty home directory), {{name}} asks our license service for the current version of your license. The request carries the license itself (which names the licensee, seats and repository scopes) and, like any network request, your IP address. The address is used only to limit request rates and is not stored. Set TESTDETTA_LICENSE_REFRESH=off to turn refresh off entirely.

Buying a license

Purchases are handled by {{seller}} as merchant of record. {{seller}} collects your payment details, billing address and tax information under its own privacy policy; we never see card details.

From {{seller}} we receive your name or company name, the email address used at checkout, the number of seats, the repository scopes you entered, and the subscription status and dates. We use them to:

  • issue your license, which carries the licensee name, seats and scopes, never an email address;
  • send the license to your email address. The address is passed to our email delivery provider for that message only and is not kept in our license records;
  • keep a record of each license and its changes, as accounting and tax law require.

Starting a trial

The trial form sends us your email address and the repository patterns you enter. We use them to:

  • email you the trial license, which carries the repository patterns and never your address;
  • send one reminder 3 days before the trial ends, unless you have subscribed by then;
  • allow one trial per organization and per address in 12 months. For this we keep a keyed hash of the address, which cannot be turned back into it, and the organizations you named.

Your IP address limits how many requests can come from one place. It is not stored.

Support

When you email us, we keep the conversation in our mailbox to help you and to answer later questions. Our email provider processes those messages on our behalf.

This website

The site uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking scripts. Our hosting provider processes request data such as IP addresses to serve pages and protect against abuse, and keeps it only briefly.

Who processes data for us

  • {{seller}}: payments, taxes and invoices (merchant of record).
  • Our email delivery provider: sending license emails.
  • Our email and hosting providers: support mail, this website and the license service.

Some of them operate outside {{licensorCountry}} and the EU. Where that is the case, transfers rely on the safeguards the law requires.

How long we keep it

  • License records: for the life of the license and as long as tax and accounting law requires afterwards.
  • Support email: as long as needed to help you, then deleted on request.
  • Trial email addresses: until 7 days after the trial ends. The hash of the address and the organizations: 12 months from the start of the trial.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) and similar laws, you can ask for a copy of your data, its correction or deletion, and object to its use. Email {{supportEmail}}. You can also complain to your data protection authority.
